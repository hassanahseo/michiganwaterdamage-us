- **Spring (March – May):** Rapid ground thaws and extended spring rainfall saturate Macomb County subsoils across the Clinton River basin. Sump pumps experience continuous operational duty cycles, with mechanical float switch failures resulting in sudden lower-level flooding.
- **Summer (June – August):** Intense localized thunderstorm downpours deliver heavy rainfall volumes over short timeframes, elevating Clinton River tributary branches and causing surface stormwater to pond in street catch basins and residential window wells.
- **Fall (September – November):** Falling autumn foliage accumulates across subdivision storm drains, creating localized street water retention during heavy rains. Early autumn temperature swings also test aging residential water heater tanks and exterior hose connections.
- **Winter (December – February):** Sustained sub-zero Arctic freeze-thaw cycles drive frost deep into suburban soils. ## Frequently Asked Questions for Sterling Heights Residents

### Why are sump pumps so critical for Sterling Heights homes?
Most Sterling Heights homes built between 1970 and 2000 have large finished basements equipped with perimeter drain tiles that direct subsurface groundwater into an interior sump pump pit. If the sump pump fails during a heavy thunderstorm or regional power outage, groundwater quickly overflows the basin and saturates finished carpets and drywall.

### How does the Clinton River watershed affect residential basement flooding?
The Clinton River and its tributary branches flow through Sterling Heights. During severe thunderstorm downpours, elevated river stages raise local groundwater tables, creating heavy hydrostatic pressure that tests foundation seals and sump pump capacities across nearby residential neighborhoods.

### What should I do if a water heater tank ruptures in my basement?
Immediately turn off the cold water shutoff valve above the water heater tank and switch the gas control valve to off. If the electrical panel is in a flooded basement, do not walk through water to reach it; call an electrician or your utility. Then call our 24/7 referral line to connect with an independent water extraction specialist and a licensed plumbing contractor.

### Can mold develop under finished basement carpet after a sump pump overflow?
Yes. Carpet padding and wood subfloors retain moisture for days after standing water is removed. If moisture is not extracted with commercial equipment within 24 to 48 hours, mold spores readily colonize the subfloor. Professional drying with industrial dehumidifiers is essential to prevent hidden mold.
