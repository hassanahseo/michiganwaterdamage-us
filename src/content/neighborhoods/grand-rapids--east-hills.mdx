## Housing Profile and Building Architecture in East Hills

East Hills is a historic neighborhood east of downtown Grand Rapids. Platted in the late nineteenth century, the area features shops and cafes along Cherry Street and Lake Drive.

Housing includes restored Victorian homes, brick rowhouses, and two-story wood homes. Many properties sit on hillside slopes with stone or brick basements.

Many historic rowhouses and storefronts were remodeled into modern homes. In these older buildings, original iron drain pipes and copper water lines connect into city mains.

City water comes from Lake Michigan through the Grand Rapids Water System. Wastewater and rain runoff drain through the city's separated sewer system.

## Local Water, Sewer, and Moisture Challenges

Because East Hills has steep hills, homes face distinct water runoff and foundation risks:

- **Hillside Storm Runoff Against Basement Walls:** East Hills sits on a sloping ridge above the downtown basin. During heavy spring rainstorms, surface water flows down driveways directly toward older foundation walls. Water presses against stone and brick mortar, seeping into basements.
- **Tree Roots in Clay Sewer Pipes:** Century-old clay sewer pipes connect homes to city street mains. Shifting hill soil and tree roots crack these vintage pipes, causing slow drains and sudden backups.
- **Hidden Water Leaks in Multi-Floor Homes:** Restored Victorian homes feature plumbing across two or three upper floors. Steady water pressure causes wear at older copper joints, creating small leaks inside plaster walls.
- **Groundwater in Low Cellars:** Homes at the base of slopes see groundwater rise during spring snowmelt. Water seeps through basement floors without dependable sump pump systems.

## 4 Critical Safety Actions During an East Hills Water Emergency

Taking structured containment steps limits property loss and keeps occupants safe:

1. **Check electrical safety first.** If your breaker panel is in a wet basement, stay out. Never walk through standing water to reach switches. Call Consumers Energy or an electrician to cut power safely from outside.
2. **Turn off the main water valve.** Locate your main domestic water shutoff valve where the city line enters your basement foundation. Turn the brass valve handle clockwise until it stops.
3. **Protect valuable furnishings.** Move furniture, rugs, and electronic equipment away from damp foundation walls or active ceiling drips. Place plastic foil squares under furniture legs.
4. **Call for 24/7 restoration or plumbing support.** For water extraction, sewage cleanup, or mold removal, call **(877) 422-0329**. For emergency plumbing and leak detection, call **(866) 487-0556**.

## Frequently Asked Questions About East Hills Property Damage

### Why do hillside homes in East Hills get basement leaks?
Steep ground slopes channel rainwater directly against foundation walls. Water presses through aging stone and brick mortar into basement cellars.

### How can homeowners direct storm runoff away from foundations?
Installing French drains and extending downspouts keeps heavy rain from pooling near foundation walls.

### Can water pumps clear standing water from old stone cellars?
Contractors use submersible pumps and water extractors to clear cellars quickly and protect structural framing.
