## Housing Profile and Building Architecture in Jefferson-Chalmers

Jefferson-Chalmers is a historic waterfront area on Detroit's lower east side, along the Detroit River and Lake St. Clair. Known as Detroit's "Venice," the neighborhood features canals that run behind residential homes. Many local families keep small boats and personal watercraft right outside their back doors.

Built largely between 1910 and 1940, the area has brick homes, bungalows, cottages, and canal houses with private boat docks and seawalls. Most houses have full basements of concrete block or hand-laid brick, while some canal homes sit on crawlspaces.

Plumbing includes copper pipes, iron waste stacks, and basement sump pumps. City water comes from the Detroit Water and Sewerage Department (DWSD). Drainage connects to Detroit's combined sewer pipes, which carry stormwater and sewage together.

---

## Local Water, Sewer, and Moisture Challenges

Because homes sit near canals and the river, Jefferson-Chalmers faces real flood risks:

- **River Surges and High Ground Water:** Sitting at river level, homes have high ground water all year. Windy weather on Lake St. Clair pushes river water over seawalls and into yards.
- **Canal Leaks and Seawall Breaks:** When river levels rise in spring, water spills over low seawalls. Floodwater enters crawlspaces, basement windows, and lower floors.
- **Sewer Overflows on Low Streets:** City sewer pipes can back up through basement floor drains when heavy rain hits.
- **Sump Pump Failures in Storms:** Because water tables remain high, home sump pumps run constantly. Power outages during storms shut off sump pumps, causing fast basement floods.

## 4 Critical Safety Actions During a Jefferson-Chalmers Water Emergency

Taking structured containment steps limits property loss and keeps occupants safe:

1. **Check electrical safety first.** If your breaker panel is in a wet basement, stay out. Never walk through standing water to reach switches. Call DTE Energy or an electrician to cut power safely from outside.
2. **Turn off the main water valve.** Locate your main domestic water shutoff valve where the municipal line enters your basement wall. Turn the brass valve handle clockwise until it stops.
3. **Avoid contact with canal floodwater.** Floodwater from canals and sewers contains harmful bacteria, fuel residues, and river contaminants. Keep children and pets away from standing water.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Jefferson-Chalmers Property Damage

### What causes severe basement flooding in Jefferson-Chalmers?
High river water and heavy rain raise groundwater levels above basement floors. Water pushes through foundation walls and overflows sump pump crocks.

### How do canal seawalls protect homes from flooding?
Concrete and steel seawalls keep normal river water from spilling onto residential properties. Broken seawalls let river water flow directly into yards.

### Can water pumps keep canal-front basements dry?
Installing dual sump pumps with battery backup power keeps water flowing out even during power outages.
