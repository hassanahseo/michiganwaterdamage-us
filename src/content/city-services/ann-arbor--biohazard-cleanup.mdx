## Wastewater Backups in Washtenaw County Properties

Wastewater problems in Ann Arbor come from river drainage and older plumbing. Many houses were built between 1900 and 1965. Older homes rely on clay or cast-iron sewer lines. These lines connect basement floor drains to city sewer mains.

The City of Ann Arbor runs a separate sewer system. Storm runoff and home waste flow through separate pipes to protect the Huron River. But during heavy summer rainstorms, rainwater can enter sanitary manholes and cracked pipe joints.

Heavy rain and melting snow soak local soil. Wet ground puts pressure on old clay sewer pipes. Shifting dirt causes clay joints to crack. Water and dirt enter the lines. When city pipes fill during storms, wastewater backs up into private lines. It then spills through basement floor drains.

Old plumbing in student rentals adds another hazard. Years of cooking grease in shared kitchens coat pipe walls. When winter cold chills foundation walls, fats harden. Combined with shifting clay soils, waste pipes clog. Sewage then backs up into cellar laundry sinks.

## Sump Overflows and Footing Drain Disconnection Challenges

The City of Ann Arbor created the Footing Drain Disconnection Program to protect the Huron River from sewer overflows. The city required thousands of homes to route foundation drains into indoor sump pits instead of sanitary sewer lines.

During severe summer storms, power outages can knock out primary sump pumps. Saturated soils outside then flood the basement crock. At the same time, stormwater in the public system creates backpressure against private lines.

Dirty blackwater pushes upward through basement floor drains. Wastewater covers floors, furnaces, water heaters, and storage boxes in harmful bacteria. Porous drywall and wood studs soak up dirty water fast. Heavy pumping and commercial cleaner washes are needed.

## Emergency Safety Measures for Property Owners

Raw drain water brings dangerous germs into your home. Follow these practical steps before help arrives:

1. **Evacuate the basement level right away.** Keep children, tenants, and pets upstairs away from standing wastewater and sewer fumes.
2. **Check electrical hazards before approaching.** If sewage reaches lower-level breaker boxes or wet outlets, stay out. Call DTE Energy or an electrician to cut power from outside.
3. **Shut the main water valve.** Turn off the main water valve if an overflowing toilet or fixture upstairs adds liquid to plugged lower pipes.
4. **Stop all indoor water use.** Do not run dishwashers, bathtubs, or washing machines. Drain water will overflow from the lowest basement drain.
5. **Call for 24/7 sewage cleanup help.** Dial **(877) 422-0329** to speak with an independent cleanup contractor serving Ann Arbor and Washtenaw County.

## The Comprehensive Five-Step Sewage Extraction and Sanitizing Protocol

Local biohazard and sewage cleanup contractors follow a strict process to return contaminated basements to safe condition:

- **Plastic Barrier Airlocks:** Contractors seal cellar stairs with plastic sheeting and zipper entries. Air scrubbers with HEPA filters trap airborne particles.
- **Pumping Liquid Waste and Sludge:** Vacuum pumps pull liquid sewage and sludge through sealed hoses for safe disposal.
- **Removing Soaked Building Materials:** Ruined drywall, damp baseboards, and wet carpet padding are bagged and hauled away under health rules.
- **Scrubbing Floors with Cleaner:** Masonry walls and concrete floors are scrubbed with deep-cleaning washes to kill bacteria and viruses.
- **Air Scrubbing and Structural Drying:** High-velocity fans and commercial dehumidifiers run until structural wood is dry.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Ann Arbor Sewage Cleanup

### How does Ann Arbor's Footing Drain Disconnection Program impact sewage backup risk?
The program disconnected foundation drains from sanitary sewers to stop river pollution, sending water to sump crocks. If a pump loses power or jams during a storm, ground runoff can back up into the cellar.

### Why is raw municipal sewage classified as a biological hazard?
Blackwater contains harmful bacteria like E. coli, parasites, and viruses. Direct contact poses health risks, requiring protective rubber gear and regulated disposal.

### How do specialists eliminate sewage smells from concrete basement floors?
Contractors remove soaked building items, extract all liquid, and wash concrete with germ-killing cleaners. Air machines run continuously to trap odor particles.

### What are landlord responsibilities when sewage floods a rental basement?
Landlords must provide alternate housing if lower living quarters or bedrooms are flooded. Fast extraction, sanitizing, and drying must be completed before tenants move back in.
