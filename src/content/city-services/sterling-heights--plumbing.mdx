## 5 Emergency Steps When You Detect a Plumbing Leak

Taking fast containment steps prevents major structural repairs and saves money:

1. **Close the main water shutoff valve immediately.** Find the main water valve where the city water line enters your basement. Turn the round handwheel or lever clockwise until water stops flowing.
2. **Keep clear of electrical hazards.** If escaping water reaches basement electrical outlets or circuit breaker panels, stay out of the room. Call DTE Energy or an electrician to shut off power from the outside meter.
3. **Open lower taps to relieve line pressure.** Turn on basement laundry tub faucets and outdoor garden spigots. This drains standing water out of the pipes safely.
4. **Turn off power to your water heater.** Switch off the dedicated circuit breaker for electric heaters, or turn gas control valves to the pilot setting.

## Precision Diagnostic and Pipe Repair Solutions

Independent plumbing contractors use modern diagnostic tools and durable repair methods to fix hidden pipe problems with minimal disruption:

- **Electronic Acoustic Leak Detection:** Plumbers place ground listening discs on concrete floors and walls. These sensors pick up the ultrasonic sound of water spraying from pinhole pipe breaks.
- **Thermal Imaging Camera Scans:** Infrared cameras display hot and cold temperature paths behind drywall and tile. This locates hidden pipe leaks without cutting open walls.
- **Push-Rod Sewer Video Inspections:** Plumbers feed high-resolution cameras through main cleanout ports. They inspect drain lines for grease clogs, cracks, and root blocks.
- **High-Pressure Hydro-Jetting:** High-velocity water jets scour away grease buildup, mineral scale, and debris. This restores full sewer flow without digging.
- **Targeted PEX Pipe Replacements:** Damaged copper or galvanized lines are replaced with flexible PEX tubing. PEX expands during freezes and resists corrosion.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Sterling Heights Plumbing

### Why do water supply lines freeze in Sterling Heights homes?
Pipes running through outside walls or drafty rim joists are exposed to winter drafts. Water inside stationary pipes freezes. It expands and splits metal pipe seams.

### What should I do if my Sterling Heights water meter continues to spin when all taps are closed?
A spinning meter dial when all taps are closed means water is escaping. Check running toilets first. If toilets are quiet, you likely have an underground supply line leak, a broken pipe under your basement floor, or an outdoor line crack.

### How do plumbers locate leaks behind bathroom tile without tearing out walls?
Plumbers use infrared thermal cameras and acoustic listening tools. These sensitive tools identify the exact temperature difference and sound frequency of spraying water behind tile before cutting begins.

### Can water heater tanks be repaired if they leak from the bottom?
When a storage water heater tank leaks from the bottom seam, internal glass lining has failed. The steel tank has rusted through. Bottom tank leaks require complete water heater replacement.
