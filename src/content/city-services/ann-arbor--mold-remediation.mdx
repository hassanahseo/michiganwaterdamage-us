## Why Ann Arbor Housing Experiences Mold Issues

Mold issues across Ann Arbor are closely tied to regional weather and older building materials. Many residential properties were built between 1900 and 1965. Historic neighborhoods like the Old West Side, Kerrytown, and Burns Park feature foundations built from fieldstone, early brick, or concrete masonry blocks.

Washtenaw County experiences humid summers. Warm outdoor air carries high moisture into cool basements. When humid air enters cellars through drafty windows, it cools quickly. This causes moisture to bead on cold stone walls, floor joists, and exposed subflooring.

The ground around Ann Arbor homes adds to chronic dampness. Glacial till mixed with river loam holds moisture near foundation walls. Water vapor passes through porous mortar seams into unfinished cellars. This raises basement humidity levels above 70 percent for weeks at a time.

Plumbing lines in older properties present added risks. Aging supply lines run from the City of Ann Arbor Water Treatment Plant & Utilities. These pipes can develop slow pinhole drips behind plaster walls. Trapped moisture feeds hidden mold inside wall framing. Stains may not appear on interior paint for weeks.

## Critical First Steps When Discovering Indoor Mold

Disturbing mold growth releases millions of tiny spores into household air. Follow these safety rules when you spot mold:

1. **Avoid disturbing mold patches.** Never scrape or scrub dry mold spots. Disturbance flings millions of invisible spores into room air. Keep hands off until containment walls are up.
2. **Shut off central air systems.** Switch your heating or cooling system fan off. This stops air ducts from blowing fungal spores into unaffected bedrooms.
3. **Block doorway airflow.** Shut the door to the damp room. Hang plastic sheeting over return air vents to isolate spores.
4. **Do not apply chlorine bleach.** Bleach fails to soak into porous wood framing or vintage masonry. It only whitens surface colors while adding water that feeds fungal roots.

## The Multi-Stage Mold Containment and Removal Protocol

Local mold cleanup contractors follow clear safety steps to clean mold without spreading spores to clean rooms:

- **Plastic Containment Barriers:** Contractors build sealed containment zones using heavy plastic sheeting. Commercial HEPA air scrubbers exhaust filtered air outdoors. This creates negative air pressure so spores cannot escape into adjacent rooms.
- **Finding Moisture Leaks:** Specialists inspect wall assemblies with thermal imaging cameras and electronic moisture meters. They find the exact source of water seepage or pipe drips before mold removal begins.
- **Safe Disposal of Soaked Materials:** Damaged ceiling tiles, wet carpet padding, and moldy drywall are carefully bagged and sealed before removal through containment airlocks.
- **Scrubbing Wood Framing:** Exposed wood joists, sill plates, and subfloor boards receive HEPA vacuuming and hand wire brushing. Contractors apply EPA-registered washes to kill active fungal roots.
- **Air Clearance Testing:** An independent air testing professional collects air samples inside the containment area. Testing confirms that indoor fungal spore counts match or beat outdoor baseline levels before barriers are removed.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Ann Arbor Mold Remediation

### Can undetected mold in a rental house hold up lease move-in dates?
Yes. Severe mold growth can violate local housing maintenance standards and create unsafe air quality. Professional containment, removal, and air clearance testing ensure living areas are safe before tenants occupy bedrooms.

### Why do stone and brick basements develop white powder along walls?
White powder on stone or concrete block is efflorescence. It forms when groundwater dissolves minerals in mortar and evaporates on interior walls. While not mold itself, it proves moisture is passing through the foundation.

### How do contractors clean mold from older floor joists in historic Kerrytown homes?
Contractors use HEPA vacuuming and wire brushing to remove surface colonies without harming old-growth timber. They apply antimicrobial washes that penetrate wood pores to kill fungal roots.

### Does landlord property insurance pay for mold removal in Washtenaw County?
Standard property insurance policies usually exclude mold unless it results directly from a covered sudden pipe break. Gradual moisture buildup and foundation seepage are typically excluded. Review your policy with your insurance agent.
