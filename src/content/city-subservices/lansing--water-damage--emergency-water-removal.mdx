## 5 Critical Safety Actions During a Lansing Water Emergency

Taking structured containment steps limits property loss and keeps building occupants safe:

1. **Check electrical safety first.** If your breaker panel is in a wet basement, stay out. Never walk through standing water to reach switches. Call Lansing Board of Water & Light (BWL) or an electrician to cut power safely from outside.
2. **Shut off the main domestic water valve.** If water comes from a ruptured supply pipe, locate the main shutoff valve. It sits where city water enters the basement. Turn the brass handle clockwise.
3. **Elevate wooden furniture above wet surfaces.** Place aluminum foil squares or plastic blocks beneath furniture legs. This stops wood stains from soaking into wet carpets.
4. **Move valuable belongings to upper floors.** Carry essential papers, family photo albums, and boxed keepsakes up to dry living areas.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Lansing Water Extraction

### Why do Lansing basements flood during Grand River crests?
High river water raises the surrounding water table. Water pressure builds against underground basement walls and forces groundwater through cracks and cold seams.

### Can waterlogged carpeting be saved after an indoor flood?
Carpeting soaked by clean domestic pipe water can often be saved if extracted within 24 to 48 hours. Water containing river mud or street runoff requires replacing carpet padding.

### How many days does commercial structural drying take in Lansing?
Commercial drying takes 3 to 5 days. Contractors measure wood moisture daily until framing lumber meets dry standards.

### Does homeowners insurance pay for emergency water extraction in Lansing?
Standard policies generally cover sudden accidental pipe bursts. Overland river flooding from the Grand River requires a separate flood policy. Sewer backups require an optional water backup endorsement.
