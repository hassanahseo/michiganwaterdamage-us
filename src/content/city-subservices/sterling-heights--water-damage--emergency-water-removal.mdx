## 5 Critical Safety Actions During a Sterling Heights Water Emergency

Taking structured containment steps limits property loss and keeps building occupants safe:

1. **Check electrical safety first.** If your breaker panel is in a wet basement, stay out. Never walk through standing water to reach switches. Call DTE Energy or an electrician to cut power safely from outside.
2. **Shut off the main domestic water valve.** If water comes from a ruptured supply pipe, locate the main shutoff valve. It sits where city water enters the basement. Turn the brass handle clockwise.
3. **Elevate wooden furniture above wet surfaces.** Place aluminum foil squares or plastic blocks beneath furniture legs. This stops wood stains from soaking into wet carpets.
4. **Move valuable belongings to upper floors.** Carry home theater electronics, personal documents, and boxed family keepsakes up to dry living areas.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Sterling Heights Water Extraction

### Why do finished basements in Sterling Heights flood during heavy rains?
Intense rainstorms saturate clay soils. Groundwater levels rise quickly. If a sump pump loses power or fails, water overflows the pit. Standing water then floods finished living areas.

### Can waterlogged carpeting in a finished basement be saved?
Carpeting soaked by clean domestic water can often be saved if pumped out within 24 to 48 hours. Water containing street runoff or sewage requires pad replacement.

### How many days does commercial structural drying take in Sterling Heights?
Commercial drying takes 3 to 5 days. Contractors measure wood moisture daily until framing lumber meets dry standards.

### Does homeowners insurance pay for emergency water extraction in Sterling Heights?
Standard policies cover sudden accidental pipe bursts. Sump pump failure requires an optional water backup rider. Review your policy with your insurance agent.
