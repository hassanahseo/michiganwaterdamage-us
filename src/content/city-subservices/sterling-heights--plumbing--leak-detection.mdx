## Warning Signs of Hidden Plumbing Leaks in Sterling Heights Homes

Concealed water leaks produce distinct signs throughout a home. Homeowners should watch for these common clues:

- **High Water Bills:** A sudden jump in utility bills from the city often points to a hidden pipe leak.
- **Warm Basement Floors:** A hot water leak under concrete slabs creates warm spots on floor tiles.
- **Damp Drywall and Peeling Paint:** Water running along pipes makes baseboards swell and paint bubble.
- **Hissing Sounds:** Hearing a faint hissing sound when all faucets are off points to a running pipe break.
- **Low Water Pressure:** Several showers losing water pressure at once can signal an active supply leak.

## 5 Critical Safety Actions When You Suspect a Water Leak

Taking organized steps limits structural water damage and keeps your property safe:

1. **Check electrical safety first.** If your breaker panel is in a wet basement, stay out. Never walk through standing water to reach switches. Call DTE Energy or an electrician to cut power safely from outside.
2. **Shut off your main water valve.** Locate the primary domestic shutoff valve where city water enters the basement near the water meter. Turn the brass valve handle clockwise until it stops.
3. **Check the water meter indicator.** Look at the small red flow triangle or digital sweep dial on your water meter. If it spins while all faucets remain off, water is actively leaking.
4. **Relocate furniture away from damp areas.** Move couches, wood tables, and area rugs away from soft drywall or damp flooring to prevent staining.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Sterling Heights Leak Detection

### How do contractors find a water leak under a concrete basement floor?
Contractors use acoustic listening discs that amplify sound frequencies from pressurized water. They also use thermal imaging cameras to track hot water pools under the slab.

### Will leak detection cause damage to my finished walls?
Modern leak detection uses non-invasive electronic tools. Contractors pinpoint the exact leak location from above without cutting open drywall unnecessarily.

### Why did my copper water pipes develop pinhole leaks?
High municipal water velocity, chemical reactions with dissolved minerals, and electrical ground currents can pit copper pipe walls from the inside out.

### Does homeowners insurance pay for leak detection and pipe repair?
Insurance policies generally cover water damage cleanup resulting from sudden pipe breaks. However, repairing the faulty pipe itself is usually considered homeowner maintenance. Review your policy with your agent.
