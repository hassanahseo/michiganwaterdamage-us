## 5 Critical Safety Actions During an Ann Arbor Water Emergency

Taking structured containment steps limits property loss and keeps building occupants safe:

1. **Check electrical safety first.** If your breaker panel is in a wet basement, stay out. Never walk through standing water to reach switches. Call DTE Energy or an electrician to cut power safely from outside.
2. **Shut off the main domestic water valve.** If water comes from a ruptured supply pipe, locate the main shutoff valve. It sits where city water enters the basement. Turn the brass handle clockwise.
3. **Elevate wooden furniture above wet surfaces.** Place aluminum foil squares or plastic blocks beneath furniture legs. This stops wood stains from soaking into wet carpets.
4. **Move valuable belongings to upper floors.** Carry textbooks, laptops, personal papers, and family keepsakes up to dry rooms.

## Professional Water Extraction and Structural Drying Steps

Independent restoration contractors follow clear step-by-step methods to pump out water and dry building materials:

## Frequently Asked Questions About Ann Arbor Water Extraction

### Why do homes in the Allen Creek watershed flood during storms?
Allen Creek flows through an underground culvert system. Extreme thunderstorms can overwhelm the pipe capacity. Water pushes upward through low street drains and enters basements.

### Can waterlogged carpeting be saved after an indoor flood?
Carpeting soaked by clean domestic pipe water can often be saved if extracted within 24 to 48 hours. Water containing street runoff or sewer waste requires replacing carpet padding.

### How many days does commercial structural drying take in Ann Arbor?
Commercial structural drying typically takes 3 to 5 days. Restoration contractors measure moisture content daily with digital meters until framing lumber meets established dry standards.

### Does homeowners insurance pay for emergency water extraction in Ann Arbor?
Standard policies generally cover sudden accidental pipe bursts. Overland flooding from the Huron River or surface runoff requires a separate flood policy. Sewer backups require an optional water backup endorsement.
