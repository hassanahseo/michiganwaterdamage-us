## Lansing Sewer Pipes and Backflow Drivers

Sewage emergencies in Lansing link to city drains. They also link to big storms. The City of Lansing runs a separated sewer system. Rain runoff and sanitary waste flow through separate pipes.

Despite separation, intense storm downpours test the system. Heavy rains and rapid spring snowmelts raise the Grand River. During high river crests, groundwater fills low-lying ground. Excess surface runoff can enter older sanitary sewer pipes through cracked lateral pipes.

When sanitary main lines fill to capacity, wastewater seeks the lowest open drain. In older areas like Old Town, Westside, and Urbandale, that point is often a basement floor drain. Raw sewage backs up rapidly. It covers laundry areas, boiler rooms, and finished basements. Without fast pumping, sewer water poses major health risks.

## 5 Critical Safety Actions During a Lansing Sewage Emergency

Taking structured containment steps keeps occupants safe and stops the spread of waste:

1. **Check electrical safety first.** If your breaker panel is in a wet basement, stay out. Never walk through standing water to reach switches. Call Lansing Board of Water & Light (BWL) or an electrician to cut power safely from outside.
2. **Evacuate the flooded area immediately.** Keep children and pets away from the basement. Raw wastewater exposes occupants to harmful germs.
3. **Stop all household water usage.** Do not run faucets, flush toilets, or run dishwashers. Any water sent down drains adds directly to the basement backup.
4. **Isolate air circulation.** Close basement doors to prevent sewer odors from reaching upper floors. Turn off central furnace fans.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Lansing Sewage Backups

### Why does raw sewage back up into Lansing basements?
During big storms, underground pipes get overwhelmed. City sewers fill with water. Trapped water pushes backward through low floor drains.

### Can basement carpets soaked with raw sewage be cleaned and saved?
No. Category 3 water contains dangerous germs that penetrate carpet fibers and padding. Carpeting and padding must be discarded to protect household air quality and health.

### Who is responsible for repairing a backed-up sewer line?
The property owner is responsible for the private sewer lateral from the house to the city main. The City of Lansing maintains the public main sewer line in the street.

### Does homeowners insurance pay for sewage backup cleanup in Lansing?
Standard policies typically exclude sewer backups unless the policy includes an optional water and sewer backup rider. Review your policy details with your insurance agent.
