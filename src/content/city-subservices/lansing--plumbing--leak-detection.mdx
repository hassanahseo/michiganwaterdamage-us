## 5 Immediate Safety and Containment Actions for Hidden Leaks

Taking structured containment steps limits water damage while waiting for plumbing tests:

1. **Check electrical safety first.** If your breaker panel is in a wet basement, stay out. Never walk through standing water to reach switches. Call Lansing Board of Water & Light (BWL) or an electrician to cut power safely from outside.
2. **Turn off the main domestic water valve.** If water meters keep spinning while all fixtures are off, close the brass valve where city water enters the basement.
3. **Relieve residual line pressure.** Open lowest-level indoor sink faucets and outdoor hose bibbs to drain trapped line water into utility drains safely.
4. **Inspect household water meters for continuous flow.** Look at the low-flow indicator dial on your Lansing Board of Water & Light meter. Continuous spinning confirms active pressurized leakage.

## Non-Invasive Professional Leak Detection Steps

Independent plumbing contractors use gentle diagnostic tools to pinpoint hidden leaks:

- **Thermal Infrared Scanning:** Contractors scan walls, ceilings, and floors with infrared cameras. They detect subtle cool spots caused by damp insulation.
- **Acoustic Ground Listening:** Plumbers use sensitive ground microphones and audio amplifiers. They listen for the high-frequency hiss of pressurized water escaping from pinholes inside walls or under slabs.
- **Pressure Decay Testing:** Plumbing supply lines are isolated and pressurized with nitrogen gas. Plumbers measure pressure loss to verify pipe integrity.
- **Video Camera Line Inspection:** Small fiber-optic cameras feed through cleanouts and pipe openings. They inspect internal drain lines for cracks or root blockages.
- **Targeted Small Access:** Once the exact leak spot is found, plumbers cut small access openings directly over the failing fitting, protecting surrounding historic walls.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Lansing Leak Detection

### How can I tell if I have a hidden water leak in my Lansing home?
Watch for unexplained increases on your municipal water bill, faint hissing sounds behind walls, warm spots on basement floors, or moldy smells in finished rooms.

### What causes pinhole leaks in Lansing copper plumbing?
Pinhole leaks typically result from internal pitting corrosion, aging soldered joints, or friction from water flow over decades of municipal service.

### Can plumbers find leaks beneath concrete floors without tearing up tiles?
Yes. Plumbers use electronic listening tools and infrared cameras to locate underground leaks through concrete slabs before making small, targeted cuts.

### Does homeowners insurance pay for professional leak detection?
Most homeowner policies cover tear-out and water damage repairs resulting from sudden pipe failures. However, they often exclude the basic cost of repairing the plumbing pipe itself. Consult your insurance representative for exact policy terms.
